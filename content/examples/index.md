Conceptual understanding of scrolling is essential, but it sticks fastest when you can see every line of a working implementation and drag it around yourself.

This section walks through the design and implementation decisions of three small, complete scrolling components — one custom `ScrollPhysics`, one custom `Viewport`, and one custom `RenderSliver`.

- To gain a higher-level understanding of the scrolling pipeline, see the [Guides](/guides) section.
- For quick recipes on individual methods and types, see the [API](/api) section.
